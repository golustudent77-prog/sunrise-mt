About Sunrise English Boarding School

Sunrise English Boarding School is a premier educational institution dedicated to providing quality education in an encouraging and nurturing environment.

Our mission is to foster academic excellence, character building, and creative thinking. We emphasize disciplined learning alongside co-curricular activities to ensure overall development.

Our Vision

To inspire students to become responsible, innovative, and successful global citizens.

Our Mission

To deliver balanced learning through modern teaching methods, strong moral values, and active sports programs.
